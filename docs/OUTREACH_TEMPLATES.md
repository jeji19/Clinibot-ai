# CliniBot AI — clinic outreach templates

Use these as starting points for individually researched, relevant clinics. Do not send bulk unsolicited messages. Respect local marketing rules and opt-out requests. Replace every bracketed field with verified information.

## English email

**Subject:** A quicker way to handle routine clinic inquiries

Hello [Name],

I noticed that [Clinic] offers [specific verified service]. I’m working on CliniBot AI, an English/Russian virtual receptionist that answers routine questions using a clinic’s approved services, prices, and opening hours, and collects appointment requests for the clinic team to review.

It does not confirm appointments or replace clinical staff. The goal is to reduce repetitive questions and make sure inquiries are captured when the reception team is busy.

Would you be open to a 15-minute demo using your clinic’s public information?

Best,  
[Sender]

## Russian email

**Тема:** Как упростить обработку типовых вопросов пациентов

Здравствуйте, [Имя]!

Я обратил внимание, что [Клиника] предлагает [конкретная подтверждённая услуга]. Мы разрабатываем CliniBot AI — виртуального администратора на английском и русском языках. Он отвечает на типовые вопросы на основе утверждённой информации клиники об услугах, ценах и графике работы, а также принимает запросы на запись для последующей проверки сотрудниками клиники.

Сервис не подтверждает запись автоматически и не заменяет медицинских специалистов. Его задача — сократить количество повторяющихся вопросов и не терять обращения, когда администратор занят.

Будет ли вам удобно посмотреть короткую 15-минутную демонстрацию на примере информации вашей клиники?

С уважением,  
[Отправитель]

## Short direct message

Hi [Name] — I’m reaching out because [specific clinic detail]. CliniBot AI answers routine clinic questions from approved clinic information and collects appointment requests for staff review, in English and Russian. It does not make medical decisions or confirm calendar slots. Would a short demo be useful?

## 15-minute demo structure

1. **2 minutes — current workflow:** ask how the clinic handles repeated questions and after-hours inquiries.
2. **4 minutes — patient experience:** test services/prices, opening hours, English and Russian, and one appointment request.
3. **3 minutes — staff workflow:** show the manager dashboard, clinic-scoped requests, status updates, and clinic-specific email notification.
4. **3 minutes — fit and limitations:** explain that requests are not confirmed appointments, no calendar sync exists yet, and medical questions are referred to staff.
5. **3 minutes — pilot decision:** agree on the clinic information owner, notification email, success measures, pilot duration, and next review.

## Follow-up

Send one concise follow-up after 3–5 business days if there is no reply. If the clinic declines or asks not to be contacted, stop outreach.

## Discovery questions

- How many routine questions about services, prices, and hours does the team handle on a typical day?
- Which channels generate the most inquiries?
- What happens to inquiries outside opening hours?
- How are appointment requests currently captured and confirmed?
- Which languages do patients actually use?
- What would the clinic need to see during a 30-day pilot to consider continuing?

Do not promise a specific reduction in staff workload or a number of additional bookings until a pilot has measured it.
